Does Do Not Disturb Block Call Box Calls?

Yes — Do Not Disturb silences call box calls just like any other incoming call. If you've ever missed a delivery because your phone was on DnD, this is exactly why. Here's what's happening and what you can do about it.

Why DnD blocks your call box

Your call box opens your gate by calling your phone — just like any other caller. When Do Not Disturb is on, your phone silences all incoming calls unless you've specifically added them to your exceptions list.

The call box doesn't know it's being silenced. It dials your number, your phone receives the call silently, times out after 20-30 seconds, and the gate stays closed. The delivery driver moves on. You get a missed call notification sometime later and wonder what happened.

The problem: Do Not Disturb treats your call box exactly like any other caller. There's no way for the system to know that this particular call is from your gate and should be allowed through.

The partial fix — adding your call box as a DnD exception

Android's Do Not Disturb settings let you create exceptions for specific contacts or numbers. If you add your call box number to your contacts and mark it as a favorite or exception, DnD will let it ring through.

Here's how to do it on Android:

  1. Save your call box number in your contacts — name it something like "Front Gate"
  2. Go to Settings → Sound → Do Not Disturb
  3. Tap Exceptions or Allow exceptions
  4. Enable Calls from contacts or add the specific number
  5. Make sure "Front Gate" is in the allowed list

This works — but it's only a partial fix. Your phone will now ring when the call box calls, but you still have to answer it manually and press 9 to open the gate. If you're asleep, in a meeting, or away from your phone, you'll still miss it.

The limitation: Adding your call box as a DnD exception means your phone rings — but you still have to answer and press 9. It doesn't solve the problem of missing the call when you're unavailable.

The complete fix — automating it entirely

The only way to truly solve the DnD problem is to remove the manual step altogether. Instead of hoping you'll be available to answer when the call box calls, you automate the response so the gate opens every time — regardless of whether your phone is on DnD, silent, or your screen is off.

Call Box Helper does this by operating as your default phone app. Because it works at the system level — not as a regular app running in the background — it receives and handles calls before Do Not Disturb can silence them. When your registered call box number calls, the app answers automatically, sends the unlock tone, and hangs up. The gate opens.

How Call Box Helper handles DnD: Because it operates as your default dialer at the system level, it intercepts call box calls before Do Not Disturb can silence them. Your gate opens automatically — even when DnD is on.

Does it work on silent mode too?

Yes. Silent mode and Do Not Disturb are handled the same way — Call Box Helper operates below both of them at the system level. Whether your phone is on silent, vibrate, or full DnD, registered call box calls are automatically answered without making a sound.

What about while my screen is off?

Also yes. Call Box Helper doesn't require your screen to be on or the app to be open. It runs as your default phone app, which means it's always active regardless of screen state. Your gate opens whether your phone is in your pocket, face down on a desk, or charging on your nightstand.

Will my regular calls still go through DnD?

Yes — your normal DnD behavior is completely unchanged for all other calls. Only numbers you register as call box numbers get the auto-answer treatment. Everything else is handled by your regular phone settings exactly as before.

Quick summary

  • Do Not Disturb blocks call box calls — your gate doesn't open if DnD is on
  • You can add your call box number as a DnD exception so it rings through
  • But you still have to answer manually — the gate doesn't open itself
  • Call Box Helper operates at the system level, below DnD and silent mode
  • With Call Box Helper, your gate opens automatically even when DnD is on
  • All other calls still follow your normal DnD rules
