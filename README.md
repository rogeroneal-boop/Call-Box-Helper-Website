# Call Box Helper

**Automatically answer your call box and open your gate — without touching your phone.**

Call Box Helper is an Android app that detects incoming calls from your building's call box, answers automatically, sends your unlock tone (DTMF), and hangs up. Your gate opens every time — even when your phone is on silent, Do Not Disturb, or your screen is off.

## Features

- Auto-answers registered call box numbers only — all other calls work normally
- Works on silent, Do Not Disturb, and while screen is off
- Customizable unlock code (supports 1-4 digits, *, and #)
- Advanced timing controls to match any call box system
- Full dialer replacement — calling, contacts, call history, mute, speaker, hold, call waiting, 3-way call
- Missed call and gate opened notifications
- Spam call detection
- Built-in setup guide

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 or higher
- Active phone service
- Apartment or gated community where the call box dials your phone number
