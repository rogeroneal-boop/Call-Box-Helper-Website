FAQ

Common questions.

What is a call box?

A call box is the intercom system at the entrance of a gated community, apartment building, or parking garage. When a visitor or delivery driver arrives, they use the call box to request entry. You, the resident, answer the call and press a number (usually 9) to open the gate. Learn more about how call boxes work →

Does it work with my call box?

If your call box dials a phone number and accepts a keypad press to open the gate, Call Box Helper will work with it. This covers the vast majority of residential and commercial call box systems.

What Android version do I need?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher. The app works best on Android 10 and above.

Does Call Box Helper replace my default dialer?

Yes. To automatically answer your call box calls, Call Box Helper needs to be set as your default phone app. This gives it the ability to detect and answer incoming calls before you have to. Your contacts, call history, and all normal calling features work exactly as before — Call Box Helper is a full dialer replacement.

Will it interfere with my regular calls?

No. The app only auto-answers calls from numbers you specifically save as call box numbers. All other calls ring normally and you answer them yourself.

Will I lose any features by switching dialers?

The Call Box Helper dialer covers all the essentials and then some — calling, contacts, call history, mute, speaker, hold, call waiting, call transfer, and 3-way call. The one thing to know is that some carrier-specific features built into your manufacturer's dialer, like visual voicemail will not carry over. For most users the transition is completely seamless.

Does it work in Do Not Disturb mode?

Yes. Because Call Box Helper is set as your default phone app, it receives calls at the system level — DnD mode does not prevent it from auto-answering your call box.

Is it free?

Free during the beta period. We'll announce pricing before the public launch, and beta testers will get advance notice.

What do beta testers get?

Beta testers get Call Box Helper free for life. If you join the beta, test the app, and send us feedback, you'll never pay — even after we launch publicly and introduce paid plans. Your help building this is worth it.

What permissions does it need?

Phone call management (to auto-answer), contacts access (to show caller names), and notifications. It does not access your microphone, camera, or location.
